Why restoration does not overwrite your saved choice

useVisitorState lazily calls loadVisitorData before rendering visitor data. The saving effect therefore receives the restored object from its first run. src/lib/storage.ts validates fields and catches storage failures. An unavailable store leaves the current session interactive.

A practice to reuse

Turn a requirement into an observable invariant before building. Here, changing sides must keep total participation at 101, and a helpful toggle must leave the seed unchanged. src/lib/domain-checks.ts asserts both kinds of behavior. Separately, scripts/render-checks.mjs checks restored content and visible UI states. On another project, this separation helps distinguish incorrect data calculations from rendering problems.
